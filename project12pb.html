Sales and Profitability Analysis

Power BI Project

  1. Home
  2. Sales and Profitability Analysis

Introduction:

This project involved building an interactive Power BI Sales Dashboard to analyze sales performance, profitability, shipping efficiency, and regional trends. The dashboard helps identify top-performing products, profitable regions, and operational insights to support data-driven business decisions.

Data Preparation:

  1. Data Loading

    The dataset was imported from a CSV file into Power Query for cleaning and transformation.

  2. Data Cleaning & Validation

    Checked the dataset to ensure there were no null or missing values, and validated the data types of all columns.

  3. Data Transformation

    Converted Order Date and Ship Date to Date format to enable time-based analysis.

  4. Column Removal

    Removed unnecessary fields such as Customer ID, Product ID, and a column in an unknown language to simplify the dataset.

  5. Data Loading to Model

    After completing transformations, the cleaned dataset was loaded back into Power BI for modeling and visualization.

Dashboard Structure:

The dashboard consists of four interactive pages designed to provide comprehensive sales insights:

  1. Sales & Profitability Overview

    High-level analysis of sales, profit, and top-selling products.

  2. Shipping & Logistics Performance

    Evaluation of shipping costs, delivery times, and shipping modes.

  3. Regional Sales Performance

    Analysis of sales and profit distribution across regions, countries, and cities.

  4. Drill-through Page

    Allows users to explore detailed product-level insights.

Measures / KPIs:

DAX queries were used to calculate all KPIs and performance metrics.

  1. Total Total Sales: $13M
  2. Total Profit: 1.47K
  3. Total Orders: $25K
  4. Profit Margin %: 11.6%
  5. Sales per Order: $505
  6. Profit per Order: $58.6
  7. Total Shipping Cost: 5K
  8. Average Shipping Cost: $283.49K
  9. Average Shipping Days: $25.86
  10. Average Delivery Days: 4
  11. Top Region by Sales: 4

Visuals:

1.Sales and Profitability:

  1. Column Chart – Average Delivery Days by Ship Mode: Compares the average delivery time across different shipping modes.
  2. Bar Chart – Top Selling Products: Highlights the products generating the highest sales.
  3. Donut Chart – Sales by Customer Segment: Shows the distribution of sales across different customer segments.
  4. Area Chart – Monthly Profit Trend: Displays how profit changes over time on a monthly basis.
  5. Waterfall Chart – Profit by Sub-Category: Illustrates how each sub-category contributes to overall profit or loss.

2.Shipping and Logistics Analysis:

  1. Bar Chart – Category-wise Sales and Profit: Compares sales revenue and profit across different product categories.
  2. Tree Map – Profit by Ship Mode and Orders by Region: Visualizes profit distribution by shipping mode and order volume by region.
  3. Bar Chart – Shipping Cost by Ship Mode: Shows which shipping modes incur the highest shipping costs.
  4. Bar Chart – Shipping Cost by Sub-Category: Highlights product sub-categories with higher shipping costs.
  5. Donut Chart – Total Orders by Ship Mode: Displays the proportion of orders delivered through each shipping mode.

3.Regional Sales Analysis:

  1. Bar Chart – Top Selling Cities: Identifies cities generating the highest sales.
  2. Map Visualization – Total Profit by Country: Shows profit distribution across different countries.
  3. Column Chart – Region vs Category Sales: Compares category-level sales performance across different regions.

Results:

  1. Top Selling Product: Apple Smart Phone ($87K) is the top-selling product, followed by Cisco ($76K), Motorola ($73K), and Nokia ($72K).
  2. Top Segment: Consumer segment contributes 51% of total sales, followed by Corporate (30%) and Home Office (18%).
  3. Top Sub-Category: Copiers ($258K) and Phones ($216K) generate the highest profits, while Tables record a loss of –$64K.
  4. Top Shipping Mode: Standard Class shipping handles over 60% of orders and generates the highest profit but has the longest delivery time (5 days).
  5. Top Selling Region: Europe and Africa generate higher profits, while New York and Los Angeles are the top-performing cities.
  6. Top Selling City: New York and Los Angeles are the top-selling cities.
