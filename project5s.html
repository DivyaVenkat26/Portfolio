Introduction:

This project analyzes FIFA World Cup match data using SQL. It aims to extract historical insights and trends, including top-scoring teams, host country performance, win/loss distribution, and more.

Data Preparation:

  1. Data Source: Raw transactional dataset of a fictional pizza chain.
  2. Data Platform: MySQL for query execution.
  3. Data Cleaning: Null handling, column renaming, formatting dates.

SQL Analysis:

      • Total Matches Played:

        • Calculates the total number of matches played across all World Cups.

        SELECT SUM(Matches_Played) AS Total_Matches_Played FROM world_cups;
      • Total Goals Scored Per Year:

        • Shows the total number of goals scored each year by adding home and away goals.

        SELECT Year, SUM(Home_Goals + Away_Goals) AS total_goals FROM world_cup_matches GROUP BY Year ORDER BY Year;
      • Most Successful Team:

        • Identifies the team with the highest number of World Cup titles.

        SELECT winner AS Team, COUNT(winner) AS Titles_Won FROM world_cups GROUP BY winner ORDER BY Titles_Won DESC LIMIT 1;
      • Top Goal-Scoring Players (2022):

        • Lists the top 5 players with the most goals in the 2022 World Cup.

        SELECT Player, Team, WC_goals AS World_Cup_Goals FROM 2022_world_cup_squads ORDER BY World_Cup_Goals DESC LIMIT 5;
      • Most Frequent Finalists:

        • Finds teams that appeared the most in World Cup finals by combining home and away finalists.

        SELECT Team, COUNT(*) AS Final_Appearances FROM (SELECT Home_Team AS Team FROM world_cup_matches WHERE stage = 'Final' UNION ALL SELECT Away_Team AS Team FROM world_cup_matches WHERE stage = 'Final') AS Final_Teams GROUP BY Team ORDER BY Final_Appearances DESC;

    KPIs:

    • Total Matches Analyzed: 900+
    • Average Goals Per Match: 2.83
    • Highest Scoring Team: Brazil
    • Most Final Appearance Team:Germany

    Insights:

    • Brazil leads in both goals scored and matches won.
    • Home advantage has significant impact — host countries have a higher win rate.
    • Germany, Brazil, and Italy have hosted more World Cup matches than other nations, highlighting their central role in tournament history.
    • Finals are most often contested by Germany, Brazil,Italy and Argentina.
    • Top Scoring Players were Thomas Muller(Germany),Cristiano Ronaldo(Portugal), Luiz Suarez(Uruguay),Harry Kane(England),Lionel Messi(Argentina)
