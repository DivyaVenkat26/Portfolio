Electric Vehicle Analysis

Power BI Project

  1. Home
  2. Electric Vehicle Analysis

Introduction:

This project involved creating an interactive Power BI dashboard to analyze and visualize Electric Vehicle (EV) data. The goal was to uncover insights on EV adoption trends, top manufacturers, model performance, and CAFV eligibility to support data-driven decisions in the electric vehicle sector.

Data Preparation:

  1. Data Loading:

    The dataset was imported into Power BI using Power Query for transformation and cleaning.

  2. Column Removal:

    Removed non-informative columns ‘VIN (1-10)’ and ‘County’ as they did not contribute to analysis.

  3. Data Type Adjustment:

    Verified and adjusted data types to ensure accuracy in calculations and visuals.

  4. Column Quality Check:

    Ensured no missing or invalid values in key columns before building visualizations.

Measures/KPIs:

DAX queries were used to calculate all KPIs and performance metrics.

  1. Total Vehicles: 150k Displays the overall count of electric vehicles in the dataset.
  2. Average Range: 67.83 Km Calculates the mean range (in miles) of all EVs.
  3. Total BEV Vehicles & BEV %: 117k,78% Shows total Battery Electric Vehicles and their market share percentage.
  4. Total PHEV Vehicles & PHEV %: 34k,22% Displays Plug-in Hybrid Electric Vehicles and their respective percentage share.

Visuals & Dashboard Layout:

  1. Area Chart: Total Vehicles by Model Year — shows EV adoption trends over time.
  2. Map Visual: Total Vehicles by State — represents geographical distribution of EVs.
  3. Bar Chart: Total Vehicles by Make — highlights manufacturer performance.
  4. Donut Chart: Total Vehicles by CAFV Eligibility — categorizes vehicles by eligibility status.
  5. Treemap: Total Vehicles by Model — identifies top-selling EV models.
  6. Slicers: Interactive filters for City, Electric Vehicle Type, and State to enable dynamic exploration.

Insights:

  1. Top Manufacturers:

    Tesla leads with 68,943 vehicles, followed by Nissan and Chevrolet.

  2. Sales Trends:

    2023 recorded the highest sales with 37,079 vehicles, showing steady growth since 2020.

  3. Historical Performance:

    Sales peaked at 14K in 2018, dropped slightly in 2019, and rose consistently from 2020–2023.

  4. Top Models:

    Tesla Model Y is the best-selling model, followed by Model 3 and Nissan Leaf.

  5. Top State:

    Washington (150,082 vehicles) recorded the highest EV count. and Nissan Leaf.

  6. CAFV Eligibility:

    41.81% vehicles are eligible, 11.6% not eligible, and 46.33% unknown.

Results:

  1. Market Growth:

    Consistent increase in EV sales highlights rising adoption of sustainable vehicles.

  2. Manufacturer Dominance:

    Tesla remains the key market leader, significantly ahead of competitors.

  3. Geographical Insights:

    States like Washington show strong EV penetration, indicating better infrastructure and awareness.

  4. Policy Impact:

    CAFV eligibility data reflects potential gaps in awareness or reporting among manufacturers and users.
